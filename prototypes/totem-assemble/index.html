Totem Arrange

Harder advanced prototype: no sockets, no labels that spoil the answer. Drag parts freely. Press Contemplate when the composition feels right — the puzzle checks relations (what signifies what), not snap targets.

Win condition is relational: e.g. “the people must stand inside the ring, with the bowl between them.” A decoy piece can sit on the stage and still fail you if it breaks the meaning — or if a required part is missing from the right relationship.
